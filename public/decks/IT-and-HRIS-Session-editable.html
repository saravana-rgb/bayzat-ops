Knowledge Hub · spaces

Spaces are how
KH stays usable.

A space is a bounded area of documentation with its own owner and audience. One space per function keeps search honest and keeps policy separate from how-to.

How a space is put together

01PagesThe content itself. A space is the container — pages are what people actually read, and they can be nested to keep structure.
02AccessEvery space and page carries permissions — who can view it, and who can edit it. Sensitive material stays scoped.
03SharingUse the share option to open a page to a person or team rather than copying it out. One source, one version.
04The IT spaceYour first stop — IT policies, acceptable use, and how-to guides for VPN, MFA and device setup.

One orchestrator,
two model families.

Orchestration

n8n

Where our workflows live. Moves data between systems, calls a model mid-flow, writes the result back. Most of what looks like "AI" here is really an n8n workflow with a model step inside it.

Reasoning

Anthropic's models. Long documents, call transcripts, drafting, code and structured deliverables. The default when there's a lot of context attached to the task.

General

OpenAI's models. General assistance and a useful second opinion. Same account rules and the same guardrails as everything else on this slide.

AI Tools

Using AI responsibly.

The short version: the AI does the first draft, you own the result.

Do

Use company accounts, never personal ones, for work

Treat every output as a draft — verify facts, figures and names

Keep a human approver on anything a customer will see

Redact or anonymise personal data wherever you can

Document automations in n8n so a colleague can maintain them

Don't

Paste customer data, salaries or medical and insurance details into a personal AI account

Let AI-written text reach a customer without a human reading it

Build automations on personal accounts that only you can access

Sign up for a new AI tool without IT reviewing the data terms

Assume a citation, statistic or quote is real — check it
